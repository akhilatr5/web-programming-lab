ICET MCA colleges are institutions in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh offering a two-year postgraduate degree in computer applications. Admission
relies strictly on state-level TS ICET or AP ICET merit ranks. The choices include a mix of prestigious government universities like Osmania,
JNTU, and Andhra University, alongside affiliated private colleges. Eligible graduates must clear the entrance exam and participat
in centralized web counseling for final seat allotment.
